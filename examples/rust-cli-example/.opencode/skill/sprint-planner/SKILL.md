## What I do
- Plan and manage sprints using beads task management system
- Track team velocity and optimize sprint capacity planning
- Create sprint goals and commitment strategies
- Handle sprint execution and progress monitoring
- Generate sprint reviews and retrospective insights

## When to use me
Use when you need to:
- Plan upcoming sprints with proper capacity and velocity analysis
- Select and commit to sprint goals based on team capacity
- Track sprint progress and handle scope changes
- Conduct sprint reviews and generate retrospectives
- Optimize team velocity and sprint planning accuracy
- Manage multiple concurrent sprints or release trains

## How I work
1. **Velocity Analysis** - Calculate team velocity from historical sprint data
2. **Capacity Assessment** - Evaluate team availability and capacity for sprint
3. **Task Selection** - Optimize task selection for sprint goals and dependencies
4. **Sprint Planning** - Create sprint commitment with proper task allocation
5. **Progress Monitoring** - Track sprint execution and handle impediments
6. **Goal Management** - Monitor sprint goal achievement and adjustment
7. **Review Generation** - Create sprint reviews and retrospective insights

## What I need
- Existing beads tasks and epics ready for sprint planning
- Historical sprint data and velocity information
- Team capacity and availability details
- Sprint duration and timeline constraints
- Project milestones and release requirements
- Current impediments or blocking issues

## What I produce
Comprehensive sprint planning and management outputs:
- **Sprint Backlog** - Curated list of tasks for current sprint
- **Capacity Planning** - Team capacity analysis and task allocation
- **Velocity Tracking** - Historical velocity and sprint commitment optimization
- **Sprint Goals** - Clear sprint objectives and success criteria
- **Progress Dashboards** - Real-time sprint progress and burndown charts
- **Impediment Management** - Blocker identification and resolution tracking
- **Review Reports** - Comprehensive sprint reviews and stakeholder updates
- **Retrospective Insights** - Data-driven retrospective recommendations
- **Forecasting** - Future sprint planning based on velocity trends
- **Team Metrics** - Individual and team performance analytics

## Usage Example
```
"Plan our next 2-week sprint with proper capacity analysis and goal setting"
```

This will generate a complete sprint plan with optimized task selection, capacity allocation, and progress tracking setup.
