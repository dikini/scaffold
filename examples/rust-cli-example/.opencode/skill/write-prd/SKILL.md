## What I do
- Guide you through creating comprehensive PRDs from scratch or existing ideas
- Use industry-standard templates with sections for problem statement, target users, success metrics, and requirements
- Validate PRD completeness and quality throughout the process
- Export PRDs to multiple formats (Markdown, PDF-ready structure)
- Ensure alignment between business goals and technical requirements

## When to use me
Use when you need to:
- Create a new PRD from an initial product idea
- Structure vague product concepts into actionable requirements
- Standardize PRD format across your organization
- Document product decisions for stakeholders and development teams

## How I work
1. **Discovery Phase** - Gather information about your product idea, target users, and business goals
2. **Structure Creation** - Build the PRD framework with all essential sections
3. **Content Development** - Fill in each section with guided prompts and examples
4. **Validation Review** - Check for completeness, clarity, and consistency
5. **Finalization** - Format and prepare for stakeholder review

## What I need
- Basic product idea or concept (even if vague)
- Information about target users or market
- Business objectives or success goals
- Any existing documentation or notes

## What I produce
A comprehensive PRD document containing:
- **Executive Summary** - Product vision and key objectives
- **Problem Statement** - Clear definition of user pain points
- **Target Users** - User personas and use cases
- **Success Metrics** - KPIs and measurement criteria
- **Functional Requirements** - Feature specifications and user stories
- **Non-Functional Requirements** - Performance, security, usability criteria
- **Assumptions & Constraints** - Technical and business limitations
- **Timeline & Milestones** - Release planning guidance
- **Risk Assessment** - Potential challenges and mitigation strategies

## Usage Example
```
"Help me write a PRD for a mobile expense tracking app for freelancers"
```

This will guide you through creating a complete PRD that development teams can use for implementation planning.
